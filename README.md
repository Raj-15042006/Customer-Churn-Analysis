# Customer-Churn-Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer churn behavior in the banking sector using PostgreSQL for data analysis and Power BI for interactive dashboard visualization.

The objective of the project was to identify customer retention patterns, understand factors contributing to churn, and generate actionable business insights through data analysis and business intelligence reporting.

The dataset consists of 10,000 customer records containing demographic, financial, and banking-related information such as credit score, balance, product ownership, activity status, salary, and churn status.

---

## Business Objective

Customer churn is a critical problem in the banking industry because customer loss directly impacts revenue, profitability, and long-term business growth.

This analysis was performed to:

* Identify customers with high churn probability
* Understand customer behavior patterns
* Analyze retention trends across regions and customer segments
* Support data-driven business decisions through visualization and reporting

## Data Quality Analysis

The dataset was checked for:

* Missing values
* Null values
* Duplicate records
* Invalid data types
* Outliers and suspicious values

### Findings

* No missing or null values were detected
* No duplicate customer IDs were found
* Data types were consistent across all columns
* Numerical values were within realistic ranges
* Minor statistical outliers were identified in Age and Credit Score

The dataset was suitable for analytical and visualization purposes.

## Power BI Dashboard Analysis

Interactive dashboards were developed in Power BI to visualize customer churn patterns and customer behavior trends.

The dashboard included:

* Executive KPI reporting
* Churn segmentation
* Customer behavior analysis
* Geography-based analysis
* Financial analysis
* Interactive filters and drill-down analysis

DAX measures and calculated columns were used to create dynamic KPIs and business metrics.

---

## Key Business Insights

* The overall customer churn rate was 20.37%
* Germany recorded the highest churn concentration despite a smaller customer base
* Inactive customers showed significantly higher churn rates
* Customers with only one banking product had the highest churn behavior
* Customers aged between 40–55 demonstrated the highest churn activity
* High-balance customers were also part of the churned segment, indicating potential revenue loss
* Multi-product customers showed stronger retention patterns

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

* Improve engagement strategies for inactive customers
* Increase cross-selling efforts to encourage multiple product ownership
* Focus retention strategies on middle-aged customer segments
* Conduct region-specific retention analysis for Germany
* Prioritize retention of high-value customers with large balances
